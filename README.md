# Global Sales Performance & Trends Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
A comprehensive 2-page Power BI dashboard suite engineered to analyze multi-year global sales performance, quarterly revenue momentum, product line distribution, customer accounts, and dealsize efficiency.

## Report Structure & Key Insights

### Page 1: High-Level Sales Trends & Overview
* **Quarterly Sales Trends:** Tracks order volume and total revenue trajectories across historical fiscal quarters.
* **Product & Deal Breakdown:** Evaluates sales volume across product lines and transaction sizes.

### Page 2: Global Performance & Target Metrics
* **YTD & Prior Year Comparison:** Evaluates Year-To-Date sales performance against established revenue goals (e.g., 584.72K target).
* **Product Line Leaders:** Identifies top revenue categories including Classic Cars, Vintage Cars, Motorcycles, Trucks, Planes, and Ships.
* **Dealsize Segmentation:** Analyzes revenue distribution across Medium, Small, and Large deal brackets.
* **Customer Account Analytics:** Ranks key global customer accounts by total revenue contribution and total order quantity.

## Tools & Technologies
* **Visualization:** Power BI Desktop (Stacked Column, Donut Charts, Line Trends, KPI Cards)
* **Data Transformation:** Power Query Editor (Date localization & M code transformations)
* **Calculations:** DAX (YTD Sales, Prior Year Benchmarks, Deal Aggregations)
